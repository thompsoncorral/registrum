Frequently Asked Questions

What is a covenant record?

A covenant record is a permanent entry that documents a horse's identity and the covenants attached to it. It includes basic information about the horse, up to two photos, and every covenant the owners have attached.

What is a covenant?

A covenant is a written commitment about how a horse will be treated, such as "no slaughter" or "standard of care." Five core covenants are attached to every horse. Owners may add optional covenants 6 through 10. You can read them all on the Registry Terms & Covenants page.

Are covenants binding?

Every owner who records a horse, and every new owner who accepts a transfer, reads the covenants, checks a box, and signs electronically. Each owner agrees to be bound by them and to require the next owner to accept them. The Registry Terms include Enforcement Terms and are governed by Arkansas law. This is not legal advice; if you have questions about your situation, please speak with an attorney.

Do I need an account or a login?

No. Nothing on the Registrum requires an account. Your horse is matched to you by its Horse ID (for example, HR-0001) and the email address you used when you recorded it.

How do I record a horse?

Click "Record a Horse," fill out the form, add a front photo (and an optional second photo), choose any optional covenants, sign, and make the recording offering ($15 for your first horse, $10 for each additional horse recorded with the same email). Once the offering is received, your horse's record becomes part of the Registrum.

What size can my photos be?

Up to two photos per horse, each 3 MB or smaller. If your phone photo is too large, most phones can share a smaller size, or you can take a screenshot of the photo and upload that.

Can I add covenants after I've already recorded a horse?

Yes. Use the Add a Covenant page with your horse's Horse ID and the email address on file. There is no charge.

Can I remove or edit covenants?

No. Covenants are designed to be permanent. A covenant that can be quietly removed is not really a covenant.

What happens when I sell or give away my horse?

The new owner starts the transfer on the Transfer Ownership page with the Horse ID and your email address on file. You'll receive an email asking you to approve or decline, and you must confirm. If you approve, the new owner receives the full covenants and must check the box and sign before making the $10 transfer offering. Both of you receive a copy of the signed agreement.

What if someone tries to transfer my horse without my permission?

Nothing changes unless you approve. Every transfer request is sent to the email address on file for the horse. If you did not agree to a transfer, click Decline or simply ignore the email.

Can I lease my horse without transferring ownership?

Yes. Use the Lease a Horse page. You stay the registered owner. After you confirm by email, the lessee reads and signs the horse's covenants, and the lease begins. There is no charge. If you set an end date, we'll remind you 7 days before it so you can extend or end the lease. The lease stays active until you end it. The horse's public record shows it is on lease, but the lessee's name is not shown.

What information is public?

The horse's name, photos, breed, color, markings, microchip number, Registrum ID, and covenants are public, so anyone dealing with the horse has notice of its covenants. You can choose whether the horse appears in the Browse Horses gallery. Your email address and contact information are never displayed publicly.

How do I update my horse's information?

Email info@horseschurch.org with your Horse ID and the changes you'd like made. Covenants cannot be removed once added.

What does my offering support?

Recording and transfer offerings help cover the cost of operating the Registrum as part of the ministry of H.O.R.S.E.S. Church.
