Registry Terms & Covenants

The Sacred Ecclesiastical Registrum of H.O.R.S.E.S. Church

1. Who we are

America's Equines (the "Registry") is operated by H.O.R.S.E.S. Church (the "Church"), a 508(c)(1)(a) faith-based organization in Arkansas. By recording a horse, adding a covenant, or accepting a transfer, you agree to these Registry Terms.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into agreements.

3. What the Registry is

The Registry is a permanent record of horses and the covenants attached to them. It is not a breed registry and does not certify bloodlines, ownership title, health, or value. A Registrum ID (for example, HR-0001) identifies a horse's covenant record only.

4. Your representations

When you record or transfer a horse, you confirm that you are its owner or are authorized by its owner, that the information you provide (including microchip number, markings, and photos) is true and complete, and that you will keep your contact information current.

5. Covenants

Covenants 1 through 5 are attached to every horse recorded in the Registry. Additional covenants (6 through 10) may be added by an owner. Once attached, covenants cannot be removed or changed by any later owner. Each owner who records a horse or accepts a transfer agrees to be bound by all covenants attached to the horse, together with the Agreement Terms below.

6. Transfers

A horse's ownership passes in the Registry only when the current owner approves the transfer, the new owner accepts the covenants and signs electronically, and the transfer offering is received. Transfers outside this process are not recognized by the Registry.

7. Public record

The horse's name, photos, breed, color, markings, microchip number, Registrum ID, and covenants are public, so that anyone dealing with the horse has notice of its covenants. Owners' email addresses and contact details are not displayed publicly. See our Privacy Policy.

8. Offerings

Recording and transfer offerings support the operation of the Registry as part of the Church's ministry. Offerings are not refundable once a record or transfer is completed.

9. Electronic signatures and records

You agree to sign and receive agreements electronically. Checking the agreement box, typing your name, and clicking the agree button is your legal signature. The Church keeps a record of each signed agreement, including names, date and time, and the transfer ID, for the life of the horse, and emails a copy to each party.

10. The Church's role

The Church is the registrar. It records the information owners provide and keeps the signed agreements for the life of the horse. It does not own, inspect, or guarantee the condition, identity, or value of any horse, and it is not a party to any sale, lease, or agreement between owners. Anyone who signed an agreement may ask for a copy of it and of the horse's record. The Church may correct errors in a record and may remove false or fraudulent entries.

11. Changes

The Church may update these Terms. Changes apply going forward and do not alter covenants already accepted for a horse.

12. Contact

info@horseschurch.org

The Covenants

In these Covenants, "Owner" means whoever is the registered owner of the Horse at the time, and "Church" means H.O.R.S.E.S. Church. Covenants 1 through 5 are attached to every horse. Covenants 6 through 10 are attached only when an owner selects them.

Core Covenants (1–5)

1. Covenants Follow the Horse. These Covenants are attached to the Horse, not to any one person. Each Owner accepts them as a condition of owning the Horse and is bound by them for as long as they own it. Before the Horse is sold, given, or otherwise transferred, the Owner must require the new owner to accept these same Covenants through the America's Equines registry. It is highly suggested that, before the Horse is leased for more than 90 days, the Owner have the lessee sign these Covenants through the registry's lease process; the Owner remains the registered owner during the lease.
2. No Slaughter. The Owner will not sell, give, trade, consign, or otherwise transfer the Horse to slaughter, or to any person or business the Owner knows or reasonably should know buys horses for slaughter, including kill buyers, kill pens, and feedlots. The Owner will not allow the Horse to be killed for human or animal consumption anywhere in the world.
3. No Export or Auction for Slaughter. The Owner will not ship or allow the Horse to be shipped to Canada, Mexico, or any other country for slaughter. The Owner will not consign the Horse to a livestock auction, sale barn, or dealer without first offering it under Covenant 6 if that Covenant is attached, and any such sale must be subject to these Covenants.
4. Transfers Only Through the Registry. Ownership of the Horse may only pass to a new owner who accepts these Covenants through the America's Equines registry. A transfer made outside the registry is not recorded.
5. Notice of Changes. The Owner will tell the Church within 30 days if the Horse moves to a new address, is lost or stolen, dies, or is euthanized, and will keep the Owner's own contact information current with the registry.

Optional Covenants (6–10)

6. Right of First Refusal. Before selling or giving away the Horse, the Owner will first offer it in writing to the Horse's previous registered owner, through the America's Equines registry. The price will be one the Owner and the previous owner agree on, or, if they cannot agree, the same price offered by the new buyer. The previous owner has 14 days to accept. If they decline, do not answer, or cannot be reached at the contact information on file with the registry, the Owner may transfer the Horse to someone else under Covenant 4.
7. Humane End of Life. If the Horse must be euthanized, it will be done humanely by a licensed veterinarian, and only for the Horse's own welfare.
8. Standard of Care. The Owner will provide the Horse with clean water at all times, adequate feed and forage, shelter from weather, room to move, routine hoof care, dental care, vaccinations and deworming as advised by a veterinarian, and prompt veterinary care when sick or injured.
9. Retirement. The Horse will not be ridden, driven, raced, or worked beyond what a veterinarian considers safe for its age and condition. Once retired by a veterinarian, it will live out its life as a companion horse.
10. Identification Kept. The Owner will keep the Horse's microchip registered to the Owner's name and will not remove, alter, or hide any microchip, brand, or tattoo.

Agreement Terms

These terms are part of every covenant agreement recorded in the Registry.

A. The Church's Role. The Church is the registrar. It records the Covenants and keeps each signed agreement for the life of the Horse. It is not a party to the Covenants and does not monitor or inspect any horse. Anyone who signed an agreement may ask for a copy of it and of the Horse's record.
B. How Long They Last. These Covenants last for the Horse's whole life and pass to each Owner in turn. An Owner's duties end once the Horse is transferred properly through the registry.
C. Electronic Signature. The Owner agrees that checking the box, typing their name, and clicking the agree button is their signature, with the same effect as signing on paper.
D. Entire Agreement. These Covenants and the Registry Terms are the whole agreement about the Horse's protections. Covenants cannot be changed or removed, but additional Covenants can be added through the Church.
